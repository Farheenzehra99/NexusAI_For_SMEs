A full-stack AI workforce platform for Pakistani SMEs built with a FastAPI backend (Python, SQLAlchemy, SQLite) and a Next.js frontend (React 18, Tailwind CSS, framer-motion, Recharts), providing six specialized AI agents — CEO, Finance, Inventory, Marketing, Customer Support, and BI — that collaborate through a deterministic orchestration layer to compute a Business Health Score, diagnose root causes, and produce prioritized action plans for the business owner.
